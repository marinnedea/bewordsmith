# Your First Page

Goal: create a new page and see it in the menu.

1. Create a file: `content/Guides/Basics/My Notes.md`
2. Start it with a top-level heading — it becomes the big title:

```markdown
# My Notes

A one-line intro here becomes the lead under the title.

## A section
Some content…
```

3. **Refresh the browser.** Done.

<div class="callout">

**Tip:** the first `# Heading` is the page title, the breadcrumb becomes the little eyebrow above it, and the first paragraph becomes the intro line in the header. You don't format the header yourself.

</div>
